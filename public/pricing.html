Why get Pro?

Say your screen flags a breakout. With Pro, you can see how far price cleared the previous high, check whether volume picked up, and look at how far the stock has fallen from past peaks. That gives you a basis for deciding whether to keep researching or move on.

  • Forecast: See the likely 10-session price range from a tested, experimental model, with a what-if for the recent trend.
  • Advanced analytics: Put returns in context with historical drawdowns, volatility, market breadth and sensitivity to Nifty 50.
  • Breakout insights: Check whether confirmed 20-day breakouts have volume behind them and see the risks you should weigh.
  • Futures & options: Find where positions cluster using NSE option chains and futures open interest.
  • Institutional flows: See whether foreign and domestic institutions were net buyers or sellers, and review reported bulk and block deals.

What can I see before signing in?

Each Pro workspace opens on one live example with real data. You don’t need an account. Choosing anything beyond the example opens the Pro offer.

  • Forecast: The 10-session range and trend what-if for Reliance.
  • Advanced analytics: Drawdown, volatility and Nifty 50 sensitivity for Reliance, with the market view.
  • Breakout insights: The full check for the latest session’s top breakout by turnover.
  • Futures & options: The Nifty option chain for the nearest expiry.
  • Institutional flows: FII and DII cash flows for the latest session, and its five largest bulk and block deals.

When is Free enough?

The free tools help you find stocks, look up a company, and organise your research. You can start without an account. Sign in with Google if you want to back up your watchlist and restore it on another device.

  • Stock search, historical charts and technical indicators.
  • Markets: See how broad a market move is, which sectors are leading and what global markets are doing alongside it.
  • Fundamentals: Assess business performance and valuation with Lodestar’s financial statements and six-pillar analysis, where data is available.
  • Company and market news.
  • Nifty 500 screening with presets, a rule builder, CSV export and up to five saved screens.
  • NSE delivery radar.
  • Watchlists, plus holdings, risk plans and a manual trade journal that stay in your browser.
  • Optional alerts and push notifications on supported devices.

What are the limits?

Public data providers may delay, throttle or withdraw access. Availability and refresh timing are not guaranteed, for free and Pro workspaces alike. Most personal research data stays in the browser; push notifications send the information needed for delivery to the server. Export a backup from Portfolio before changing devices or clearing browser storage.

Does Alpha Nova charge for broker handoffs?

No. Brokerage, exchange charges, taxes and other broker-side costs are separate. Review the broker’s own terms and final order details. A handoff does not confirm execution or place a protective stop automatically.

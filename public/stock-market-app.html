Use the app on desktop, Android or iPhone

Visit tickervane.vercel.app in a modern browser. The workspace adapts to your screen, and you can use it directly without an app-store download. An internet connection and JavaScript are required for market data and interactive features.

  • iPhone or iPad: open the site in Safari and use Share → Add to Home Screen. Home-screen installation is required for web push on supported iOS devices.
  • Android: use your browser’s Install app or Add to Home screen option when available.
  • Desktop: keep the app in a browser tab, or use the browser’s installation option if offered.

Start with one company

  1. Select Find instrument and search a company or ticker.
  2. Review its quote timestamp, chart and company headlines.
  3. Add it to your watchlist, then use the screener or delivery radar to investigate related opportunities.
  4. Export a backup from the Portfolio workspace to keep a copy of your local research data.

Frequently asked questions

How do I move my saved research to Alpha Nova?

On your previous device or website address, open Portfolio → Backup & restore and export a backup. Open tickervane.vercel.app and import that file in the same workspace. Earlier AlphaNova backups are supported. Browser data and notification permissions are specific to each website address, so enable push notifications again on Alpha Nova.

Is the stock market app free?

Alpha Nova’s research workspace is free to use. Public market data can be delayed, throttled or unavailable. Your broker’s separate charges and terms apply if you continue to a brokerage service.

Does Alpha Nova execute trades?

Research and planning happen in Alpha Nova. The planner can hand off a ticket to supported broker workflows, but you must review and confirm any order with your broker. Alpha Nova does not verify fills or automatically update your journal from broker activity.

Will my watchlist sync across devices?

Watchlists, holdings and journal entries are stored in the browser on your device. Export and import a backup to move them. Enabling push sends the subscription and alert settings needed for notifications to the server; it is not account-based cloud sync.

Privacy & sign-in

Google sign-in

If you choose “Sign in with Google”, Google shares your name, email address and Google account identifier with Alpha Nova. We verify your Google identity on our server and store the account privately with Vercel. We do not receive your Google password or request access to Gmail, Drive, contacts or calendars. Signing in does not subscribe you to marketing emails.

A secure, HttpOnly cookie keeps you signed in for up to seven days. A separate short-lived cookie protects the sign-in request. Session records are stored privately; signing out deletes the current session and clears these cookies. Sessions expire after seven days even if their stored record has not yet been removed. Account records remain until the site operator removes them. We do not store Google sign-in tokens in browser storage, analytics or URLs.

Alpha Nova Pro payments

If you unlock Alpha Nova Pro, we store the UPI reference number you enter, a short payment note derived from your account (also added to your UPI payment), the amount, the time you submitted it and your access period against your Google account, privately with Vercel. The site operator checks each reference against the bank record of the receiving account. Alpha Nova does not receive your UPI PIN, bank account number or card details; your UPI app and bank handle the payment. Your browser also remembers the date your access ends.

Name and email

If you choose “Sign up for free”, Alpha Nova stores your name, email address, registration date and the version of the notice you submitted. These records are stored privately with our hosting provider, Vercel, and are accessible to the site operator. We do not publish a directory of registered users.

Registration does not subscribe you to marketing emails. The optional name/email form does not verify your email or authenticate an account; Google sign-in is separate. Entering an email never gives access to someone else’s details or research records.

We save your welcome name and registration time in this browser so we can greet you and stop showing the signup prompt. We do not put your name or email in analytics events or page URLs.

Your research records

Watchlists, holdings, trade journals, planner drafts and preferences are stored in your browser. Signing out does not erase local research. Clearing browser storage can remove it; use Portfolio → Backup & restore to export a copy.

If you choose “Back up watchlist” after signing in with Google, we store your list of instrument symbols and the backup time privately with Vercel against your Google account. Backups can be restored or deleted from your account. They do not update automatically and do not include holdings, trades, alerts or planner drafts.

Optional push notifications

If you enable push notifications, we also store your browser’s push subscription, watchlist, price alerts, saved screens, notification preferences and a short notification history on Vercel so alerts can work when the app is closed. This is separate from free registration.

Usage and performance

Alpha Nova uses Vercel Analytics and Speed Insights for page usage, feature events and performance measurements. Hosting and external services also process the network requests required to serve the app. Google Fonts supplies the app’s fonts, and market and news services supply research data.

Alpha Nova uses the Google Ads tag to measure how visitors reach the site from ads. Google may use cookies and receive information such as the page URL and ad click identifiers. See how Google uses data from partner sites.

Your choices

You can dismiss the signup prompt and continue browsing. A dismissal pauses automatic prompts for seven days on that browser. Google sign-in is optional and available from the header. The free research tools remain available without signing in; Alpha Nova Pro requires Google sign-in.

“Forget my name on this browser” removes the local welcome name only. It does not delete your stored registration. Registration records remain until the site operator removes them. To request removal, contact the founder through the contact links on the About page and identify the email address you registered. Ownership may need to be confirmed before a request is fulfilled.

Privacy & free registration

Name and email

If you choose “Sign up for free”, Alpha Nova stores your name, email address, registration date and the version of the notice you submitted. These records are stored privately with our hosting provider, Vercel, and are accessible to the site operator. We do not publish a directory of registered users.

Registration does not subscribe you to marketing emails. Your email is not verified, and registration is not an authenticated account. Entering an email never gives access to someone else’s details or research records.

We save your welcome name and registration time in this browser so we can greet you and stop showing the signup prompt. We do not put your name or email in analytics events or page URLs.

Your research records

Watchlists, holdings, trade journals, planner drafts and preferences are stored in your browser. Free registration does not upload or sync these records between devices. Clearing browser storage can remove them; use the app’s backup and restore feature to keep a copy.

Optional push notifications

If you enable push notifications, we also store your browser’s push subscription, watchlist, price alerts, saved screens, notification preferences and a short notification history on Vercel so alerts can work when the app is closed. This is separate from free registration.

Usage and performance

Alpha Nova uses Vercel Analytics and Speed Insights for page usage, feature events and performance measurements. Hosting and external services also process the network requests required to serve the app. Google Fonts supplies the app’s fonts, and market and news services supply research data.

Your choices

You can dismiss the signup prompt and continue browsing. A dismissal pauses automatic prompts for seven days on that browser. The header signup button remains available.

“Forget my name on this browser” removes the local welcome name only. It does not delete your stored registration. Registration records remain until the site operator removes them. To request removal, contact the founder through the contact links on the About page and identify the email address you registered. Ownership may need to be confirmed before a request is fulfilled.

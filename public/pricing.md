# Alpha Nova pricing

Updated: 2026-10-03
Canonical page: https://alphanova48.in/pricing.html

Price: INR 0. No subscription or Alpha Nova account required for the current browser app.

Includes charts, Nifty 500 screening, CSV export, delivery radar, end-of-day derivatives research, available flow data, watchlists, risk plans and a manual journal. Up to five saved screens. Optional alerts require a supported device.

Public data can be delayed, throttled or unavailable; no availability or refresh guarantee. Personal research data is device-local by default. Push notifications send required alert information to the server. Broker charges and third-party costs are separate. Handoffs do not confirm execution or automatically transmit protective stops.

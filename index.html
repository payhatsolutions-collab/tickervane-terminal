Alpha Nova: stock prices, charts and market research

Alpha Nova, also written as AlphaNova, is a free browser-based stock market research app for Indian and global markets. It brings historical price charts, technical indicators, company headlines, a Nifty 500 screener and an NSE delivery radar into one workspace.
